Live Navigation (opt-in GPS)

Uses your browser's live location (navigator.geolocation) to route to a destination via this cluster's self-hosted OSRM (repositorium) and re-route as you move. Nothing starts until you click "Start navigation" — the browser will then prompt for location permission. No live traffic data (OSRM has none) — ETAs are road-network/speed-limit based only. Position is used live for routing only; nothing about it is logged or persisted server-side, and tracking stops the moment you click Stop or leave this page.
